# 🎤 EduCore Presentation Guide

Use these points to deliver a compelling 10-minute demo of the platform.

---

## 1. The Vision (1 Minute)
*   **The Problem**: Academic administration is often "reactive"—we only notice a student is failing after they've already missed their exams.
*   **The Solution**: EduCore is a "proactive" platform. It uses AI to support students and data analytics to alert administrators early.

## 2. Student Experience: The AI Agent (2 Minutes)
*   **Demo Point**: Navigate to the Student Dashboard -> AI Study Agent.
*   **Highlight**: "This isn't just a chatbot; it's a context-aware academic assistant."
*   **Feature**: Use the **Voice Input** (Mic) to ask a question like *"Explain the basics of Operating Systems."*
*   **Value**: Show the **Attendance Heatmap**. It makes attendance visual, helping students self-correct.

## 3. Faculty Efficiency (2 Minutes)
*   **Demo Point**: Navigate to Faculty -> Timetable Generator.
*   **Highlight**: "Manual timetable scheduling takes days. EduCore does it in seconds."
*   **Feature**: Show the smart logic (30-min breaks every 2 hours).
*   **Value**: Mention the **Study Material Hub**—a single source of truth for all course content.

## 4. Admin Intelligence (3 Minutes) - *THE WOW FACTOR*
*   **Demo Point**: Navigate to Admin Dashboard.
*   **Highlight**: "This is the Command Center."
*   **Feature 1: Behavioral Risk Matrix**: Show the Treemap. Explain that it automatically flags students with <75% attendance. This is the **early warning system**.
*   **Feature 2: AI Engagement**: Navigate to AI Engagement. "We are tracking the 'AI Literacy' of our campus." Show which subjects are using AI the most.
*   **Feature 3: Verification**: Show the "Pending Verifications" for leave requests. Everything is centralized.

## 5. Technical Excellence (1 Minute)
*   **Architecture**: Built with **Vite + Supabase** for sub-second responses and real-time synchronization.
*   **Light Mode Design**: Optimized for bright classroom environments with high-contrast, premium aesthetics.
*   **Scalability**: Ready for Vercel deployment with optimized TypeScript build pipelines.

## 6. Conclusion & Future (1 Minute)
*   "EduCore isn't just a management tool; it's an intelligence layer that ensures no student is left behind."

---

### 💡 Quick Pro-Tips for Demo:
1.  **Start in the Admin Dashboard**: It has the most visual "wow" factor with the treemaps and counters.
2.  **Use the Login Shortcuts**: Use the bottom-row buttons on the login page to switch between roles quickly.
3.  **Mention RLS**: If asked about security, mention that the platform uses **Row Level Security (RLS)** in PostgreSQL to ensure students can't see each other's private data.
